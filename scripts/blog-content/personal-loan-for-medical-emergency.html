A medical emergency does not wait for payday. Hospital deposits, surgery, medicines and follow-up care can run into lakhs within days, and health insurance does not always cover everything. A personal loan for a medical emergency can fill the gap quickly, but in a stressful moment it is easy to make an expensive choice. This guide lays out your options in the order that usually costs least, and how to borrow quickly and safely.

First, use what you already have

Health insurance and cashless treatment

If you or your employer has health insurance, ask the hospital's insurance desk about cashless treatment at the start. For planned procedures, get pre-authorisation. Keep all bills and reports, as you may be able to claim reimbursement for expenses not settled cashless.

Employer support

Some employers offer salary advances, medical loans or additional group cover. Ask HR; these are often cheaper than any outside loan.

Savings and fixed deposits

An emergency is what an emergency fund is for. A loan against your fixed deposit is another option if you do not want to break it, and usually costs less than an unsecured loan.

How to get the loan faster

  • Start with your own bank. Many banks offer pre-approved personal loans to existing customers, sometimes disbursed within hours through net banking.
  • Keep documents ready: PAN, Aadhaar, the last 3 months' salary slips or ITR, and 6 months' bank statements. See our personal loan documents guide.
  • Ask for the amount you actually need, based on the hospital's estimate, plus a small buffer.
  • Apply with a co-applicant if your income or score is weak. Read co-applicants explained.
  • Do not apply to many lenders at once. Each hard enquiry can lower your score and multiple rejections slow things down.

Stay safe from scams in a crisis

Families in a hospital emergency are a target for fraud. Be very careful of:

  • Callers who offer an "instant medical loan" for an advance fee
  • Apps that ask for access to your contacts and photos
  • Anyone asking for OTPs or remote access to your phone
  • Crowdfunding or "donation" requests that ask you to pay first

A genuine lender discloses all charges in the Key Fact Statement and disburses directly to your bank account. Read our guide to loan app fraud and RBI rules.

Tax benefits to ask your CA about

Personal loan interest is generally not tax-deductible for medical use. However, health insurance premiums and certain medical expenses for specified illnesses or senior citizens may qualify for deductions under the Income Tax Act, depending on the tax regime you choose. Ask a chartered accountant what applies to you.

After the emergency: rebuild your safety net

  • Pay every EMI on time to protect your credit score
  • Prepay the loan when the insurance claim or savings allow
  • Rebuild an emergency fund of several months of expenses
  • Review your health insurance: is the sum insured enough? Do you need a top-up or super top-up plan?

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a personal loan in one day for hospital bills?

It is possible, especially for pre-approved bank customers with a good credit score and complete documents. It is not guaranteed; approval and timing are the lender's decision.

Can the loan be paid directly to the hospital?

Personal loans are usually credited to your bank account, and you pay the hospital. Some medical finance schemes pay the hospital directly.

What if my credit score is low?

A secured option such as a gold loan, FD loan or loan against car may be easier. See personal loans with a low CIBIL score.
