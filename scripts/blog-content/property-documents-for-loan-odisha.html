When you apply for a loan against property or a home-related loan, the lender's lawyer and valuer will examine your property papers in detail. Missing, outdated or mismatched documents are the most common reason property loans get delayed or rejected. This guide lists the property documents for a loan in Odisha, explains what each one proves, and shows how to fix common gaps before you apply.

Requirements differ between lenders and property types, so use this as a checklist and confirm the final list with your lender.

Why lenders check property papers so carefully

The property is the lender's security. Before lending, it must be satisfied that:

  • You (and any co-owners) legally own the property
  • The title is clear, with no disputes or existing loans
  • The land is of a type that can be mortgaged, for example homestead or converted land
  • Buildings on it are approved and the property can be sold if needed

Two checks run in parallel: a legal check by the lender's advocate and a technical check by its valuer, who visits the property.

Core ownership documents

Registered sale deed and the chain of title

The registered deed by which you acquired the property, whether sale deed, gift deed, partition deed or settlement deed, plus earlier deeds showing how ownership passed over the years. Lenders often ask for a title history going back several years, sometimes 13 years or more.

Record of Rights (RoR)

In Odisha, the Record of Rights, often called the khatian or patta, shows the recorded owner, plot number, khata number, area and the kisam (type or classification) of land. You can view land records on the Odisha government's Bhulekh portal. The name on the RoR should match the name on your deed.

Mutation

Mutation updates the land records in your name after you buy or inherit the property. If you bought land but never applied for mutation, the RoR may still show the previous owner. Apply for mutation at the Tahasil office before you apply for a loan.

Encumbrance certificate (EC)

An EC shows registered transactions on the property, such as sales and mortgages, over a period. It helps the lender confirm there is no existing registered charge. In Odisha, ECs are issued through the registration department, and an online service is available through the Inspector General of Registration (IGR) Odisha website.

Land classification and conversion

Many lenders will not accept agricultural land as security for a loan against property. If your land is recorded as agricultural but is used for a house or shop, you may need to get it converted to non-agricultural use under the Odisha Land Reform rules. Conversion is handled through the revenue authorities. Check the kisam on your RoR early, because conversion can take time.

Building approvals

  • Approved building plan from the relevant authority, for example the development authority or municipal body in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack or your town
  • Occupancy or completion certificate, where applicable
  • For flats: the builder's approvals, the society or association documents, and ORERA registration details for newer projects

Construction that deviates significantly from the approved plan can reduce the loan amount or lead to rejection.

Tax and utility documents

  • Latest property tax or holding tax receipts
  • Land revenue (khajana) receipts
  • Electricity bill in the owner's name, helpful as supporting proof

Documents for special situations

Inherited property

Legal heir certificate or succession certificate, the death certificate of the previous owner, and a registered partition or relinquishment deed if there are several heirs. All heirs who have a share may need to be co-applicants or give consent.

Jointly owned property

All co-owners usually need to be co-applicants on the loan. See co-applicants and guarantors explained.

Property with an existing loan

If the property is already mortgaged, a new lender may take over the loan as a balance transfer. You will need the current lender's statement of outstanding balance and list of documents held.

Power of attorney

If someone signs on the owner's behalf, a registered power of attorney is usually needed. Lenders check these closely.

Common problems and fixes

  • Name mismatch between deed, RoR and Aadhaar: get the records corrected or provide an affidavit, as the lender advises
  • Mutation pending: complete it before applying
  • Agricultural kisam: start the conversion process early
  • Missing old deeds: certified copies can be obtained from the sub-registrar's office
  • Unpaid taxes: clear dues and keep receipts

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a loan on land without a house?

Some lenders accept vacant non-agricultural plots, often with lower LTV. Agricultural land is generally not accepted for a loan against property.

Do I need to submit original documents?

For a mortgage loan, the lender typically keeps the original title deeds until the loan is repaid. Get a list of documents held, signed by the lender, and collect every original at closure.
